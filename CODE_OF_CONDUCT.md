# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

This project is committed to a respectful, harassment-free collaboration
environment for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected Behavior

1. Be constructive and specific in technical feedback.
2. Assume good intent, but hold work to clear quality standards.
3. Keep discussions on-topic and focused on the project.
4. Share context when raising bugs, concerns, or disagreements.

## Unacceptable Behavior

1. Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
2. Doxxing or sharing private information without consent.
3. Repeated disruptive behavior after maintainer requests to stop.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or restrict participation for
violations. Serious or repeated violations can result in temporary or permanent
bans from project spaces.

## Reporting

Report conduct issues to project maintainers through repository discussions or
private contact channels used by the maintainers.
